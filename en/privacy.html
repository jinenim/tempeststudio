Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Tempest Studio ("we") processes the personal information of users of our games and services ("Services") as described below, in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea and other applicable laws. This policy applies to Dice Poly and all other games we publish on Google Play and other stores. If this English version differs from the Korean version, the Korean version prevails.

1. Information we collect

  • Guest login: an app-generated device identifier and your game account number, created automatically on first launch.
  • Google sign-in: your Google account ID and email address, received from Google when you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Identity and age verification (19+ games): the verification result (whether you are an adult) and a duplicate-registration or connecting identifier (DI/CI), through a licensed Korean identity verification provider.
  • Purchases: product, time, order number and receipt data through Google Play Billing. We never receive your card or other payment method details.
  • Use of the Services: nickname, game records and currency history, access times, IP address, device model, OS version and error logs.
  • Support requests: your email address and the content of your inquiry.

2. How we use it

  • To identify you, sign you in, and link or recover your account
  • To verify identity and age and apply legal limits such as monthly purchase caps
  • To run the game and matchmaking, deliver purchases and process refunds
  • To prevent and act on fraud, hacking and abuse
  • To answer inquiries, send notices, and improve the Services

3. Retention

We delete your information without delay when you delete your account or when it is no longer needed, except that we keep: records of contracts, payments and delivery for 5 years and records of consumer complaints or disputes for 3 years (Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce), and access logs including IP addresses for 3 months (Protection of Communications Secrets Act).

4. Sharing

We do not provide your personal information to third parties unless you consent or the law requires it.

5. Processors and international transfer

  • Amazon Web Services, Inc.: server hosting and storage, in the Republic of Korea (Seoul region).
  • Google LLC: Google sign-in and in-app payments; data is transferred over the network to Google data centers, including in the United States, when you use these features, and kept under Google's policies.
  • Identity verification provider: identity and age verification, in the Republic of Korea. We will name the provider here before the official launch.

6. Your rights

You may at any time ask to access, correct, delete or stop the processing of your personal information, or delete your account, from the in-game settings or by emailing us at the address below.

7. Children

Dice Poly is rated 19+ and is available only to adults verified through identity verification. In our other games we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.

8. Security

We encrypt data in transit, limit access to personal information, keep and review access logs, and store credentials in encrypted form.

9. Cookies

Our game apps do not use web cookies. This website does not use cookies or visitor tracking tools.
